Finish installing Autoum

The local companion is installed. For each browser you selected in Setup:

  1. Open the browser’s menu → Extensions → Manage extensions.
  2. Turn on Developer mode, then choose Load unpacked.
  3. From your Start menu, open Autoum Extension → Chrome extension folder or Vivaldi extension folder. Copy that folder’s address and select it in the browser’s folder picker.
  4. Open Autoum’s Details and enable Allow access to file URLs for local lessons and reports. Pin Autoum to the toolbar.

Click Autoum to open the sidebar, then connect your accounts in Settings. Each browser keeps its own accounts and chats; your existing new-tab page stays as it is.

Updating

Close the browsers you selected, run Setup again, then click Reload on Autoum’s Extensions card. Your accounts and chats are preserved.
